About me

I am an entry-level Application Developer from Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu, with strong fundamentals in Python, SQL and modern web technologies. I completed my Master of Computer Applications in 2024 with a CGPA of 9.59, after earning a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science with a university rank and Gold Medal.

My internship at Techvolt Software Private Limited gave me hands-on experience in full-stack development. There I built web applications with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python and MySQL, followed the Software Development Life Cycle, and collaborated with teammates using Git and GitHub.

My academic projects reflect that range: a secure biometric hashing module for banking, a MERN-stack grocery store with user authentication, and a responsive travel website built in React.js. Each one is live online.

I enjoy writing clean, object-oriented code, designing RESTful APIs and turning ideas into usable products. I am now looking for an opportunity where I can contribute, keep learning and grow as a developer.
